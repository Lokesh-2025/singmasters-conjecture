OBSERVATIONS
================================================================
1. Transpilation overhead (161×) is the dominant challenge on heavy-hex topology
2. Pass manager optimization (opt level + seed sweep) is the highest-ROI fix
3. Oracle simplification via shared Boolean conditions cuts gates significantly
4. RCCX safe for ancilla compute/uncompute but NOT for phase kickback oracle
5. Removing barriers between circuit sections enables compiler boundary cancellation
6. Hardware amplification (4.17×) close to simulator prediction (6.2×) at 103 gates
7. Phase 4: jumping from 4→7 qubits increases transpiled gates 6× even at 1 iteration
8. A3 ancilla (not a separate APH) works as phase kickback target: H|1>=|-> trick
9. Hardware noise floor: ~600 gates. Above that, signal collapses regardless of algorithm
10. Routing overhead scales with qubit degree: degree-4 hub → 4× gate inflation
11. Graph embedding is the real constraint — not gate count, not algorithm design
12. LNAA breakthrough: Ising Hamiltonian quantum walk achieves 27.78× with 135 gates
    — hardware-native gates (RZZ+RX) need zero routing, so logical ≈ physical gate count
    — encode targets as ground states, not Boolean conditions
    — 6.7× better than previous hardware record (4.17×, Phase 3v3)
13. Automatic collision discovery: encode_collision_problem finds C(n1,k1)=C(n2,k2) pairs
    from just k1,k2 inputs — zero manual circuit design, 122× amplification
14. Parallel rails: 3 simultaneous searches in ONE hardware job, 300× amplification
    — ibm_kingston 156 qubits used 30 (9 per rail), room for 10+ parallel searches
    — shorter circuits per rail = less noise = better hardware/simulation ratio

    — sieve_singmaster_space validated: found 3003 at all 8 known positions correctly
